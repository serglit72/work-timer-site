A day with Working Timer

One screen that changes with your day.

Today knows where you are in the shift. It shows only the next thing you need — and steps out of the way the rest of the time.

Every day laid out from 6 AM to 6 PM. Tap a bar for the route, tap a date to change it.

Anything past 8 hours gets a red outline and an “OT” tag — so you can move a stop, not argue a timesheet.

Planning

Plans change. Reorder in a tap.

Swap the order of sites with the arrows next to each arrival time. Working Timer rebuilds the day around the new route — arrival times, drive time and when to leave home.

Move sites up or down

Office moves to 8:00 AM, Harbor Yard shifts to 1:20 PM. Time on site stays, everything else follows.

Work log

Review. Fix. Send. Done.

Every day the app logged, in one list. Check it, correct what’s off, and send the week where it needs to go — without retyping a single hour.

Review at a glance

Paid hours per day and for the week, overtime flagged, and days that still need a look marked in orange.

Edit with a paper trail

Adjust in and out times per site. The original GPS time stays on record and the entry is marked as edited.

CSV, ready for Excel

Date, site, address, in, out, breaks, drive and hours — one row per entry, opens cleanly in a spreadsheet.
